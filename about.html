Our Story

From Supply Chain Consulting to Comprehensive Solutions

Simbus Technologies was founded in 2010 with a clear vision — to simplify complex business processes using intelligent technology.

What started as a focused initiative in supply chain consulting has evolved into a comprehensive solutions company delivering capabilities across supply chain planning, data engineering, and AI-driven analytics.

Today, Simbus Technologies stands as a trusted partner for companies looking to modernize their operations and build resilient, data-driven enterprises.
