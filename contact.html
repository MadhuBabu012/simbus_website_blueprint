FAQ

Common Questions

How quickly can you start an engagement?

We can typically onboard and start a project within 1–2 weeks of contract signature. Our Expert Pod model allows for even faster deployment when urgency requires it.

Do you offer fixed-price or time & materials engagements?

We offer flexible engagement models including fixed-price projects, time & materials, managed services (AMS), and our Expert Pod model — whichever aligns best to your business needs.

What industries do you serve?

We work across manufacturing, FMCG, retail, e-commerce, pharma, and logistics — any enterprise with complex supply chain or data challenges.

Are you a certified Kinaxis and Databricks partner?

Yes. Simbus Technologies is a trusted Kinaxis implementation partner with 100+ projects delivered. We also have deep Databricks implementation expertise across multiple industries.

Do you provide ongoing support after implementation?

Absolutely. Our Application Maintenance & Support (AMS) services ensure your Kinaxis and Databricks platforms continue to perform optimally with L1, L2, and L3 support coverage.
